No outside shareholders or overcomplicated ownership or management structures steer the investment process.

FINMA Licence

FINMA-authorised · Manager of collective assets

Z22 holds one of Switzerland's demanding financial-market licences: we are authorised and directly, prudentially supervised by FINMA as a manager of collective assets (Verwalter von Kollektivvermögen).

The licence requires, among other things, an appropriate organisation, minimum capital and collateral, dedicated portfolio management and risk management, and the guarantee of irreproachable business conduct and the necessary expertise of those in charge (Art. 7 ff. FINIG). Only a small number of managers of collective assets exist in Switzerland - and fewer still combine this standing with genuine expertise across both classic assets and digital assets. That combination is at the core of what we do.

Why Resilience

From the static 60/40 to a dynamic all-weather portfolio

The classic 60/40 portfolio rests on a single assumption - that bonds rise when equities fall. 2022 showed how fragile that is, when both fell together. Dynamic Resilience instead allocates systematically across six liquid asset classes - equities, government bonds, commodities, gold, bitcoin and cash - through low-cost ETFs, and reweights them continuously by market trend. A genuine all-weather base portfolio: stability first, with additional return opportunities across every market regime.

Why Opportunity

Uncorrelated returns beyond long-only asset classes

Traditional diversification runs out exactly when you need it - in a crisis, correlations converge and everything falls together. Dynamic Opportunity is a systematic, diversified portfolio of liquid alternative and quantitative strategies, engineered to deliver genuinely uncorrelated returns across market cycles. Why Iron Balls

More Bitcoin through trading - dual compounding in USD

A permanent Bitcoin core, made to work harder. Iron Balls holds a constant Bitcoin base and layers systematic overlays on top - trend-following, systematic option selling, cross-exchange arbitrage and volatility harvesting. The trading steadily grows the number of bitcoins held; because investors also participate in Bitcoin's price, the return compounds twice in USD (dual compounding) - more bitcoins, at a potentially higher price. Fully systematic and data-driven, with no discretionary intervention.

Why Smart Mirror

US equities with a built-in tail hedge, not gut feel

Stay invested in the US market - without betting the outcome on your nerves in a crash. Smart Mirror combines a permanent core in S&P 500 ETFs with a systematically implemented tail hedge. When the models detect tail risk, the strategy builds rule-based exposure to VIX futures (via long-VIX ETFs, "Z22 VolaBoost"), typically for a short, defined period. Fully quantitative and rule-based; discretionary intervention is excluded, for consistent, unemotional execution.

Frequently asked questions

What is Z22 Technologies?

Z22 Technologies is a Swiss, FINMA-regulated systematic quant asset manager and an official spin-off of ETH Zürich and the University of St. Gallen (HSG). Founded in 2019, we are 100% founder-owned and independent, building all-weather and absolute-return strategies in liquid markets - driven entirely by algorithms, never by discretion.

Is Z22 Technologies regulated?

Yes. Z22 holds a FINMA authorization as a manager of collective assets and is supervised by the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA). Learn more about the licence: FINMA - manager of collective assets.

Who is behind Z22 Technologies?

Z22 was founded by Philipp Baumann (MA Business Management, University of St. Gallen) and Armin Herbsthofer (MSc Physics, ETH Zürich). The Board of Directors comprises Prof. Dr. Josef Teichmann (Financial Mathematics, ETH Zürich), Thomas Hochstrasser (Niederer Kraft & Frey) and Thomas Hirsiger (Finiens Wealth Management). Severin Lehmann (SwissComply AG) heads the firm's Risk & Compliance Management.

Who owns Z22 Technologies?

Z22 is owned 100% by its two founders, Philipp Baumann and Armin Herbsthofer. With no outside shareholders or external dependencies, we focus entirely on our clients and our strategies.

How are your interests aligned with mine?

Z22 is 100% founder-owned, and the founders invest their own capital in the same strategies offered to clients. We sit in the same boat: when clients do well, so do we. No outside shareholders or third-party product agenda steer the process.

What investment strategies does Z22 offer?

Z22 runs a platform of liquid, systematic strategies split into Core and Satellite solutions. Core: Z22 Dynamic Resilience (all-weather base portfolio), Z22 Dynamic Opportunity (market-neutral absolute return) and Z22 Iron Balls (Bitcoin strategy with systematic overlays). Satellite: Z22 Smart Mirror (US equity with a statistical tail hedge) and C22 Crypto Strategy (systematic crypto trend-following).

What does "all-weather" mean at Z22?

Our all-weather approach (Z22 Dynamic Resilience) allocates systematically across several liquid asset classes - equities, government bonds, commodities, gold, Bitcoin and cash - via low-cost ETFs, reweighting them continuously by market trend. The goal is stability first, with added return opportunities across all market phases, rather than relying on a single 60/40 assumption.

Who can invest, and what is the minimum?

Clients invest through their own bank: any standard securities account (Depot) can access our liquid strategies, so there is no separate account to open. Our products are intended exclusively for professional, qualified and institutional investors. Minimums and terms vary by product and are detailed in each factsheet - for specifics, contact sales@z22.ch.

Is there a contract between me and Z22 Technologies?

No, and that is a deliberate advantage for you. You invest through your own bank, so there is no direct contract with Z22, no complex onboarding and no lengthy due diligence, KYC or documentation on our side. You keep your existing banking relationship and decide how much - or how little - to share with us. You also stay fully in control: all our strategies are liquid - the daily ones trade at any time, and our fund vehicles offer weekly or monthly liquidity. There are no multi-year lock-ups, notice periods or complex private-market contract structures. To exit, you simply instruct your own bank to sell - no need to tell us, cancel a contract or observe a notice period; the position is sold at the next dealing date.

Can I access Z22 strategies through my wealth manager?

Yes. Many clients invest through their wealth manager or external asset manager (EAM). We support intermediaries with full documentation and direct access to the team for due diligence, and our strategies can be placed as core or satellite building blocks in USD, CHF or EUR.

How liquid are the strategies?

Liquidity is defined per strategy: Z22 Dynamic Resilience and Z22 Smart Mirror are daily, Z22 Dynamic Opportunity and Z22 Iron Balls are monthly, and C22 Crypto Strategy is weekly.

Is Z22 a wealth manager for private clients?

Z22 is not a classic wealth manager but a FINMA-regulated, quantitative asset manager (manager of collective assets). For high-net-worth private clients and entrepreneurs, however, our strategies play exactly the role many look for in wealth management: a systematically managed core portfolio plus targeted satellite building blocks - transparent, liquid and rule-based instead of gut-driven. You invest directly in the strategies or through your existing wealth manager.

What happens when I get in touch?

You speak directly with the people who run the strategies, not a call centre. We start with a personal needs assessment, tell you honestly whether and where we fit, and only then help you select the right strategy or combination. The first step is a conversation, with no obligation.
